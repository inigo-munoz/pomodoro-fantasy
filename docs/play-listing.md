# Google Play listing

Draft copy for the store listing, in English and in Spanish (Spain). Limits: app name 30
characters, short description 80, full description 4,000. Every claim below matches what the app
does today; keep it that way when the app changes (no ads, no accounts, no data collected).

## English

**App name** (16): Pomodoro Fantasy

**Short description** (75): Study in short blocks and raise a magical creature that grows as you focus.

**Full description**

Pomodoro Fantasy turns study time into a gentle adventure. Pick a magical creature, start a
focus block, and watch it grow as you work.

**Study in short blocks**
- Focus for 15 minutes, then rest for 5. After a few blocks, enjoy a longer break.
- Choose your own times: 10, 15 or 25 minutes of focus, or anything you like.
- Calm music while you study: cosy tunes, lofi, an enchanted harp or the sounds of nature.

**Raise your creature**
- Sixteen creatures in four families: dragons, cats, unicorns and wolves.
- Each one hatches, grows and becomes an adult as you study and feed it.
- Every finished block earns coins to spend on food and on decorating your creature's lair.

**Quests that cheer you on**
- Dozens of quests that celebrate what you have done, and three new ones every week.
- A record of your study days, week by week and month by month.

**Made for children**
- No ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.
- Nothing you do leaves your device: your game is saved on the device only.
- Make a backup file of your game whenever you like, and restore it on this or another device.
- Works offline.

## Español (España)

**Nombre de la app** (16): Pomodoro Fantasy

**Descripción breve** (71): Estudia en bloques cortos y cría una criatura mágica que crece contigo.

**Descripción completa**

Pomodoro Fantasy convierte el rato de estudio en una aventura tranquila. Elige una criatura
mágica, empieza un bloque de estudio y mira cómo crece mientras trabajas.

**Estudia en bloques cortos**
- Estudia 15 minutos y descansa 5. Después de unos cuantos bloques, disfruta de un descanso más largo.
- Elige tus tiempos: 10, 15 o 25 minutos de estudio, o los que quieras.
- Música tranquila mientras estudias: melodías acogedoras, lofi, un arpa encantada o sonidos de la naturaleza.

**Cría a tu criatura**
- Dieciséis criaturas en cuatro familias: dragones, gatos, unicornios y lobos.
- Cada una nace, crece y se hace adulta mientras estudias y le das de comer.
- Cada bloque terminado te da monedas para comprar comida y decorar la guarida de tu criatura.

**Misiones que te animan**
- Decenas de misiones que celebran lo que has hecho, y tres nuevas cada semana.
- Un registro de tus días de estudio, semana a semana y mes a mes.

**Pensada para niños y niñas**
- Sin anuncios, sin cuentas y sin compras dentro de la app.
- Nada de lo que haces sale de tu dispositivo: la partida se guarda solo en él.
- Haz una copia de seguridad de tu partida cuando quieras y recupérala en este u otro dispositivo.
- Funciona sin conexión.

## Notes for the Play Console

- **Target audience:** children (Families policy). Declare: no ads, no data collected or shared,
  no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- **Data safety form:** "No data collected"; "No data shared". The backup file is created and
  sent only when the user chooses to, to a place the user picks.
- **Permissions to explain:** notifications (a block or break has ended) and keeping the screen
  awake during a block.
- **Privacy policy URL:** https://inigo-munoz.github.io/pomodoro-fantasy/privacy.html (English and Spanish; `public/privacy.html`). Also shown as text on the in-app Credits screen.
